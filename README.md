# global-retail-sales-analysis
global retail sales analysis using excel & tableau

Business Problem
Retail businesses often struggle to understand which regions, products, and pricing strategies drive profitability. This project analyzes global retail sales data to identify key revenue drivers, optimize discount strategies, and improve overall business performance.

# Objective
- Identify high-performing regions and markets  
- Analyze product category profitability  
- Evaluate impact of discounts on sales and profit  
- Track Year-over-Year (YoY) growth trends  
- Provide actionable insights for pricing and expansion strategies  

# Dataset Overview
The dataset includes:
- Order Date  
- Region / Country  
- Product Category  
- Sales Revenue  
- Profit  
- Discount  

# Tools & Technologies
- *Excel* → Data cleaning, transformation, preprocessing  
- *Tableau* → Dashboard creation and visualization
  
# Analysis Performed
# Revenue Trend Analysis
- Evaluated sales performance over time  
- Identified seasonal trends and growth patterns  

# Regional Performance Analysis
- Compared revenue across regions and countries  
- Highlighted top-performing and underperforming markets  

# Product Category Analysis
- Analyzed profitability by product category  
- Identified high-margin vs low-margin products  

# Discount Impact Analysis
- Studied relationship between discount levels and sales  
- Evaluated impact on profitability  

# Dashboard Features
- Interactive sales trend visualization  
- Region-wise performance comparison  
- Category-level profitability insights  
- Discount vs Sales analysis  
- Dynamic filtering for deep-dive analysis  

# Key Insights
- Certain regions contributed significantly to overall revenue growth  
- Higher discounts increased sales volume but reduced profit margins  
- Specific product categories consistently outperformed others  
- Underperforming markets indicated potential for targeted improvement  

# Business Recommendations
- Optimize discount strategies to maintain profit margins  
- Invest in high-performing regions for expansion  
- Improve performance in low-performing markets through targeted campaigns  
- Focus on high-margin product categories
